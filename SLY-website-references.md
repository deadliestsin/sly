## Ideas for SLY

- **3D logo hero:** the SLY logo as a real bent chrome or powder-coated rod that turns as you scroll.
- **Shelving configurator:** drag modules and swap colours in 3D, then send the setup with "Contact now".
- **Café scene:** a small explorable 3D room with the shop, café counter and products on the shelves.
- **Performance:** keep 3D to one or two signature moments so the site stays fast on phones.
