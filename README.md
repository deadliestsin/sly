# SLY Website

Redesign of [sly-design.com](https://www.sly-design.com), moving off Squarespace to a custom site.

**SLY** is a design store, showroom and Italian café in one. The products used in the café (cups, cutlery holders, shelving, desk organizers) are part of the SLY product line and can be bought. Designed and made by Denis, an industrial designer.

## Goals

- A bold, gallery-like look in the spirit of &Walsh, Studio Dumbar, Koto and Snarkitecture
- One or two signature 3D (three.js) moments, such as a 3D logo hero or a shelving configurator
- Stays fast on phones
- Sells the products and speaks to partners and investors
